About - Beliefs

Jesus is Lord. The Bible is truth. Grace lifts us higher. We believe in salvation, healing, the Holy Spirit, and the power of prayer. John 3:16, Romans 5:20.

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, He died for our sins, He rose again, and He is alive today. We believe the Bible is the true Word of God, it guides us, teaches us, and gives us life. We believe grace is greater than sin, grace forgives, grace heals, grace restores, and grace lifts a person higher than before. We believe in salvation by grace through faith, not by our works. We believe God still heals today, body, mind, heart, and family. We believe the Holy Spirit fills us, leads us, comforts us, and gives us power to live for God. We believe prayer changes things, prayer opens doors, prayer brings peace, and prayer brings miracles. We believe every person is loved by God, welcomed at the Fountain, and called to grow, serve, and reach one more person for Jesus.

About - History

Started in 2024 in Badege Layibi, Gulu with a small gathering under Pastor Sam Otema. Today we are a growing fountain for families in Layibi and beyond.

In 2024, Pastor Sam Otema answered the call of God to start a church in Badege Layibi, Gulu. We started small, with a few families, in a simple gathering, with a big heart for God and for people. We prayed together, we worshipped together, we shared the Word together, and we loved one another like family. From that small beginning, God has grown us. Today we are a growing fountain for families in Layibi, in Gulu, and beyond. Every Sunday families come to worship, to learn, to pray, and to grow in grace. Every week cells meet in homes. Every month we reach out to the community. We are still growing, still believing, still reaching one more soul. This is only the beginning of what God will do through FOHGM.

About - Accountability

Every gift is stewarded with integrity. Finances are accounted for, leadership is accountable, and the church is open.

At FOHGM, we believe every gift belongs to God and must be handled with honesty, care, and transparency. When you give, your gift is recorded, it is prayed over, and it is used for the work of God in Badege Layibi, Gulu. Finances are accounted for, leadership is accountable, and the church is open to questions. Pastor Sam Otema and the leaders serve with a clean heart, with integrity, and with love for the people. We do not hide anything. We believe in doing things in the light, with truth, with order, and with respect for every giver. Your trust is precious to us, and we will protect it.

About - FAQs

Where is the church? Badege Layibi, Gulu, Uganda. Fountain of Higher Grace, near the community of Badege Layibi. Come and you will be welcomed like family.

When are services? Sunday 9:00 AM - Main Service, Worship, Word, and Prayer. Wednesday Prayer 6:00 PM - Midweek Prayer, come and pray with us. Friday Bible Study 6:00 PM - Learn the Word together, grow in grace, ask questions, and share life.

Who is the pastor? Pastor Sam Otema, Founder and Senior Pastor since 2024. Phone 0771755800. Pastor Sam loves God, loves people, loves to pray with families, visit homes, and teach the Word with grace and truth.

Can I come if I am new? Yes. Come as you are. You do not need to be perfect to come. You will be welcomed, loved, and prayed for. Bring your family and friends.

What should I wear? Wear what you have. Come clean and comfortable. God looks at your heart, not your clothes.

Do you have programs for kids? Yes. Kids are loved at FOHGM. They learn, sing, pray, and grow in a safe and happy place.

About - Privacy Policy

We respect your information. We never share your prayer requests or personal details without permission. Your name is used only to welcome you and to follow up on your request.

At FOHGM, we protect your privacy with love and respect. When you give us your name, email, phone, prayer request, testimony, or giving record, we keep it safe. We never share your prayer requests or personal details with others without your permission. Your name is used only to welcome you by your last name on the website, and to follow up on your request with prayer and care. Your prayer is sacred. Your story is precious. We will not sell your information. We will not post your testimony without asking you first. If you ever want us to delete your message, just tell Pastor Sam and we will do it. You are safe here at the Fountain.
