About FOHGM

Established 2024 at Gulu Bardege Layibi - Fountain of Higher Grace Ministries

About FOHGM - Our Full Story

1. Fountain of Higher Grace Ministries (FOHGM) was established in 2024 in Bardege Layibi, Gulu City. It began as a small prayer fellowship of only 7 people meeting in a home with one desire: to drink from a Fountain where grace is higher than sickness, higher than barrenness, higher than poverty, and higher than every report of man. Today, by the grace of God, that small fellowship has grown into a family church where hundreds have found home, healing, and hope.

2. Our name is our assignment. A fountain never runs dry, it flows out to thirsty people. Higher Grace means the grace of God that lifts a person above what doctors said, above what years of waiting said, above what family background said, and above what the enemy planned. At FOHGM, we do not preach a small grace. We preach Higher Grace that breaks barrenness, opens wombs, heals bodies, restores marriages, provides jobs, and turns shame into testimony.

3. We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled, prayer-driven church. Our tagline is Steadfast in Faith, Boldness in Impact. Our motto is Liberate, Equip, Send. We liberate souls from sin, fear, curses, and delay through the Word, prayer, and deliverance. We equip saints with deep teaching, prayer life, and covenant understanding. And we send them as Oracles to impact families, schools, workplaces, businesses, and nations.

4. Our community is our family. From Sunday celebration to Thursday midweek, from pastoral home visits to phone prayer and counselling, every member is known, loved, and prayed for by name. No one is a stranger at FOHGM. Whether you are coming from Gulu Town, Bardege, Layibi, Pece, Laroo, or far away, you will find fathers, mothers, brothers, and sisters who will stand with you until your testimony comes.

5. Our vision is big because our God is big. We see 500 members strong, our own land, a permanent hall in Bardege Layibi, a training school for Oracles, a media team that carries Higher Grace to the whole world, and a generation that is steadfast in faith and bold in impact. We are building not just a church building, but a Fountain that will flow to the next generation.

6. Our values are Faith, Boldness, Grace, Family, Prayer, Covenant, Love, and Excellence. We believe in prayer that gets answers, giving that opens doors, worship that touches heaven, and a Word that transforms lives. We believe every delay has an expiry date, every barren womb will carry, every sick body will be healed, and every forgotten person will be remembered by God.

7. If you are thirsty, come and drink. If you are weary, come and rest. If you are broken, come and be mended. If you are lost, come and find home. FOHGM is not just a church in Gulu. It is a Fountain. And the water is still flowing. Welcome home to Fountain of Higher Grace Ministries.

About Pastor Otema Samuel

Name: Pastor Otema Samuel
Title: Oracle of God, Covenant Practitioner
Calling: Liberate, Equip, Send
Vision: Raise generation steadfast in faith, bold in impact

Pastor Otema Samuel is the founder and Senior Pastor of FOHGM. He is called as an Oracle of God - a voice that hears God and speaks life over barrenness, sickness, poverty, and delay. He is a Covenant Practitioner who teaches the power of covenant, prayer, giving, and Higher Grace. His ministry is marked by powerful prayer, deep Word, deliverance, and testimonies of babies after many years, healings, jobs, and restored families. He believes no one should die in waiting. He pastors with love, with boldness, and with a father's heart for Gulu and for the nations.

Our Statement of Faith

1. The Bible: We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. It is our final authority for faith, life, and ministry. Every teaching at FOHGM is tested by the Word.
2. One God: We believe in One God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Creator of heaven and earth, holy, loving, and just.
3. Jesus Christ: We believe Jesus is the Son of God, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose again on the third day, and is seated at the right hand of God. He is Lord and Saviour.
4. Salvation: We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone. Whoever believes and confesses Jesus is saved, forgiven, and made a new creation.
5. Holy Spirit: We believe in the Holy Spirit, His power, His gifts, His fruit, and His leading. He empowers us to live holy, to pray, to witness, and to manifest Higher Grace.
6. The Church: We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, called to Liberate, Equip, and Send. We are a family, a fountain, and an army for God in Gulu and beyond.
7. Higher Grace: We believe in Higher Grace - the grace that breaks barrenness, heals sickness, cancels debts, opens doors, and turns years of waiting into testimony. Grace is higher than every report.

Our Mission

Our Mission is to Liberate souls, Equip saints, and Send Oracles. We liberate every soul from sin, fear, barrenness, sickness, and poverty through the Word, prayer, and deliverance. We equip saints with sound teaching, a strong prayer life, covenant understanding, and Higher Grace. We send them as Oracles to impact families, workplaces, schools, businesses, and nations. Our mission is not to gather a crowd, but to raise a generation that will carry the Fountain to the world. Every member at FOHGM is called to be a carrier of Higher Grace.

Our Services

Come and experience Higher Grace. Everyone is welcome. Bring your family, bring your prayer request, bring your faith.

More About Our Services

Our Sunday Main Service is a celebration of worship, undiluted Word, testimonies, and prayer for all needs. Thursday Midweek is deep teaching, prayer, and deliverance for those who want to grow deeper. Pastoral Visits bring the church to your home - Pastor Sam prays with your family, anoints your house, and stands with you in your situation. Phone Prayer and Counselling is for those who cannot come physically - your request goes straight to the Admin Dashboard and Pastor prays for you and replies. Every service is designed to liberate you, equip you, and send you with Higher Grace.
