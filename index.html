Meet Pastor Sam Otema

SO

Pastor Sam Otema

Founder & Senior Pastor, FOHGM

Pastor Sam Otema is a servant of God called to preach higher grace, healing and restoration. Through SAM Ministry, he leads FOHGM in Kampala with a heart for prayer, the Word, and helping families rise in faith. His message is simple: No matter where you have been, grace can lift you higher.

📖 "Where sin abounded, grace did much more abound." - Romans 5:20

About FOHGM

FOHGM is a grace-filled Christian community in Kampala, Uganda, under the leadership of Pastor Sam Otema. We exist to share the love of Jesus, build strong families, and raise disciples who carry grace to their homes and nations.

Vision: To be a fountain where everyone drinks from higher grace.
Mission: Worship, Word, Prayer, and Compassion.
