About - Vision And Values

We are a cell-based community church in Badege Layibi, Gulu, celebrating Christ, growing and multiplying as each one reaches one; touching those around us with the love of Jesus, bringing healing to the cities and the nations.

Values: Grace, Prayer, Word, Family, Outreach, Integrity

About - Beliefs

Jesus is Lord. The Bible is truth. Grace lifts us higher. We believe in salvation, healing, the Holy Spirit, and the power of prayer. We believe God loves you, forgives you, and has a beautiful plan for your life. John 3:16, Romans 5:20.

About - History

Started in 2024 in Badege Layibi, Gulu with a small gathering under Pastor Sam Otema. Today we are a growing fountain for families in Layibi and beyond. Every Sunday, families come together to worship, learn, pray, and grow in grace.

About - Accountability

Every gift is stewarded with integrity. Finances are accounted for, leadership is accountable, and the church is open. We believe in honesty, transparency, and serving God and people with a clean heart.
