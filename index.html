VISION

We are a cell-based community church in Badege Layibi, Gulu, celebrating Christ, growing and multiplying as each one reaches one; touching those around us with the love of Jesus, bringing healing to the cities and the nations.

About FOHGM

Fountain of Higher Grace - Badege Layibi, Gulu. Established in 2024.

Our Beliefs

Jesus is Lord. The Bible is truth. Grace lifts us higher. We believe in salvation, healing, the Holy Spirit, and the power of prayer.

Our History

Started in 2024 in Badege Layibi, Gulu, with a small gathering under Pastor Sam Otema. Today we are a growing fountain for families in Layibi and beyond.

Accountability

We steward every gift with integrity. Finances are accounted for, leadership is accountable, and the church is open.

Leadership

Meet the shepherd of the Fountain

SO

Pastor Sam Otema

Founder & Senior Pastor - Fountain of Higher Grace

Pastor Sam Otema was called to start Fountain of Higher Grace in Badege Layibi, Gulu in 2024. His heart is for the lost, the broken, and the next generation. Through the Word, prayer and higher grace, he is raising a church that reaches one to reach one.

📖 Romans 5:20 - "Where sin abounded, grace did much more abound."
