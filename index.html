About Us

FOHGM is a grace-filled Christian community in Kampala, Uganda. We exist to share the love of Jesus, build strong families, and raise disciples who carry grace to their homes, workplaces and nations.

Our Vision: To be a fountain where everyone drinks from higher grace.

Our Mission: Worship, Word, Prayer, and Compassion in action.
