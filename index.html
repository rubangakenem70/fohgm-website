About FOHGM

Established in 2024 at Gulu Bardege Layibi - Fountain of Higher Grace Ministries

About FOHGM

1. Fountain of Higher Grace Ministries (FOHGM) was established in 2024 in Bardege Layibi, Gulu City. It began as a small prayer fellowship of only 7 people meeting in a home with one desire: to drink from a Fountain where grace is higher than sickness, higher than barrenness, higher than poverty, and higher than every report of man. Today, by the grace of God, that small fellowship has grown into a family church where hundreds have found home, healing, and hope.

2. Our name is our assignment. A fountain never runs dry, it flows out to thirsty people. Higher Grace means the grace of God that lifts a person above what doctors said, above what years of waiting said, above what family background said, and above what the enemy planned. At FOHGM, we do not preach a small grace. We preach Higher Grace that breaks barrenness, opens wombs, heals bodies, restores marriages, provides jobs, and turns shame into testimony.

3. We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled, prayer-driven church. Our tagline is Steadfast in Faith, Boldness in Impact. Our motto is Liberate, Equip, Send. We liberate souls from sin, fear, curses, and delay through the Word, prayer, and deliverance. We equip saints with deep teaching, prayer life, and covenant understanding. And we send them as Oracles to impact families, schools, workplaces, businesses, and nations.

4. Our community is our family. From Sunday celebration to Thursday midweek, from pastoral home visits to phone prayer and counselling, every member is known, loved, and prayed for by name. No one is a stranger at FOHGM.

5. Our vision is big because our God is big. We see 500 members strong, our own land, a permanent hall in Bardege Layibi, a training school for Oracles, a media team that carries Higher Grace to the whole world, and a generation that is steadfast in faith and bold in impact.

6. Our values are Faith, Boldness, Grace, Family, Prayer, Covenant, Love, and Excellence. We believe in prayer that gets answers, giving that opens doors, worship that touches heaven, and a Word that transforms lives.

7. If you are thirsty, come and drink. If you are weary, come and rest. If you are broken, come and be mended. If you are lost, come and find home. FOHGM is not just a church in Gulu. It is a Fountain. And the water is still flowing. Welcome home to Fountain of Higher Grace Ministries.

Pastor Otema Samuel is the founder and Senior Pastor of FOHGM. He is called as an Oracle of God - a voice that hears God and speaks life over barrenness, sickness, poverty, and delay. He is a Covenant Practitioner who teaches the power of covenant, prayer, giving, and Higher Grace.

Our Statement of Faith

1. The Bible: We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God.
2. One God: We believe in One God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
3. Jesus Christ: We believe Jesus is the Son of God, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died on the cross, rose again, and is seated at the right hand of God.
4. Salvation: We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
5. Holy Spirit: We believe in the Holy Spirit, His power, His gifts, His fruit, and His leading.
6. The Church: We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, called to Liberate, Equip, and Send.
7. Higher Grace: We believe in Higher Grace - grace higher than every report.

Our Mission

Our Mission is to Liberate souls, Equip saints, and Send Oracles. We liberate every soul from sin, fear, barrenness, sickness, and poverty through the Word, prayer, and deliverance. We equip saints with sound teaching, a strong prayer life, covenant understanding, and Higher Grace. We send them as Oracles to impact families, workplaces, schools, businesses, and nations.

Our Sunday Main Service is a celebration of worship, undiluted Word, testimonies, and prayer for all needs. Thursday Midweek is deep teaching, prayer, and deliverance. Pastoral Visits bring the church to your home. Phone Prayer and Counselling is for those who cannot come physically.
