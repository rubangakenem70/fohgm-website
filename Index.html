Our Mission, Vision, Purpose

Our Mission

To Liberate souls, Equip saints, Send as Oracles

Our Mission is to LIBERATE every soul from sin, fear, barrenness, sickness, poverty through Word, prayer, deliverance. We EQUIP saints with teaching, Higher Grace, prayer life. And we SEND them as Oracles to impact families, workplaces, schools, businesses and nations.

Our Vision

To be Fountain of Higher Grace where people drink grace that breaks barrenness, heals, prospers

Our Vision is to see a Fountain in Bardege Layibi where thousands drink Higher Grace - grace higher than medical reports, higher than years of waiting. We see 500 members, own land, permanent hall, training school.

Our Purpose

To be Fountain where people drink Higher Grace

Our Purpose is to be a Fountain that flows out - thirsty drink, weary rest, broken mended, lost find home. Core Values: Faith, Boldness, Grace, Family, Prayer, Covenant, Love, Excellence.
